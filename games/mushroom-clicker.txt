<h2>Description</h2>
A game about farming mushrooms with the help of witches, spaceships, and the Mario brothers.
<h2>Links</h2>
<a href="https://orteil.dashnet.org/igm/index.html?g=https://pastebin.com/raw/ys0Tdq9p">Game Link</a>
<h2>Details</h2>
<p>The game follows a typical incremental format, with buildings that produce mushrooms and upgrades that make mushroom production more effective.</p>
<p>The game was developed as a submission for the <a href="https://itch.io/jam/lazy-jam-autumn-2024">Lazy Developer Autumn 2024 Game Jam</a>, and was seemingly the only entry.</p>
