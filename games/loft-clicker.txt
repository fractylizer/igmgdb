<h2>Details</h2>
Based on the Roblox game Loft Tower 2. "Loft" is short for Licking Soft, and "Woft" is short for Water Licking Soft. They are characters from Loft Tower 2. A <a href="https://acompleteidiot.github.io/loft-clicker-2">sequel to Loft Clicker</a> created with JavaScript was released in September 2026, and the original was then relocated to GitHub.
