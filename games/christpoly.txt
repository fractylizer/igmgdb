<h2>Description</h2>
A unique board-like game where you purchase land, upgrade them and, hopefully, get paid. Has unique features like a Respect System, unstable and stealable land blocks and custom bots that can play against you!

<h2>Details</h2>
The game was created for the <a href="./?p=igm-game-jam-2025">IGM Game Jam 2025</a>, and placed #3 out of 5 games submitted. It is inspired by the board game Monopoly and its name is a portmanteau of Christmas and Monopoly.
