<h2>Details</h2>
<p>Stone Mine Simulator (formerly known as "Mine!") was created in late 2019 after a series of small, experimental clicker games. The game was primarily inspired by a science class LG had back in middle school, and was originally planned to have an entire system for traveling deep into caves.<br>The game was most known for having a strange mix of stock images and pixel icons, but it also had a great amount of buildings and upgrades. It received several updates through the rest of 2019, though the game would eventually cease all development soon into the next year.</p>
<p>After years of abandonment, Stone Mine Simulator was officially restored in 2024, along with its seemingly lost CSS. Unfortunately, a good chunk of the game's icons is no longer available due to link rot and changes to Piskel.</p>
