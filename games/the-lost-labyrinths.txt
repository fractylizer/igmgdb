<h2>Description</h2>
This is an RPG-style game, where you fight different monsters, get better and better gear, brew potions to enhance your stats, and eventually... you'll reach the Labyrinth.

<h2>Game Details</h2>
<p>The Lost Labyrinths is a RPG game where you fight monsters to earn loot. The game was made to rival games like Forez's Dream, and development started in late September 2022.<br>After a month of work, The Lost Labyrinths was officially released. It featured four monsters to fight, fishing and mining, and one labyrinth to explore. The game would continue receiving updates through 2022 and into 2023, and the last Alpha version was released in mid-2023.</p>
<p>Development would slow down greatly after the release of Alpha 1.2, and the first planned Beta version would never end up seeing the light of day. The last patch was released in 2024, which made the update names more consistent with LG's other games.</p>
<p>This game was posted in DashNet, r/idlegamemaker, and LG's personal site.<br>This game is also featured in Episode 2 of <a href="?a=irondizaster">IronDizaster</a>'s <a href="?p=irondizaster-pyigmg">Playing Your Idle Game Maker Games</a> series.</p>
