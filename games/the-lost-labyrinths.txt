<h2>Description</h2>
This is an RPG-style game, where you fight different monsters, get better and better gear, brew potions to enhance your stats, and eventually... you'll reach the Labyrinth.<br>

<h2>Game Details</h2>
The Lost Labyrinths is a RPG game where you fight monsters to earn loot. Battles are real time. This game was posted in DashNet, r/idlegamemaker, and LG's personal site. It was also featured in Episode 2 of <a href="/?a=irondizaster">IronDizaster</a>'s Playing Your Idle Game Maker Games series.
