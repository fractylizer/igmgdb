<h2>Description</h2>
A point-and-click horror game where you work in the depths of Santa's workshop, fighting against what lurks in the shadows. Make it through the last five days 'til Christmas for that sweet holiday bonus. And maybe some therapy.

<h2>Details</h2>
<p>After Hours X is primarily inspired by FNAF 1 and FNAF 3, featuring a gameplay loop similar to that of FNAF 1's. There are five nights that become increasingly harder as you play, a bonus night featuring a surprise guest, and a custom night where you can tinker with the difficulty as you please.<br><br>Development of After Hours X began right in the middle of finals week for LG, yet he managed to hit the ground running. The game was submitted on December 25 at 4 in the morning, and was officially released on December 29.<br><br>After Hours X is most notable for being the first horror game made in IGM. Due to the two-week deadline of the game jam it was made for, an entire section section of gameplay was scrapped early in development.<br>The game was created for the <a href="./?p=igm-game-jam-2025">IGM Game Jam 2025</a>, and placed #1 out of 5 games submitted.</p>
