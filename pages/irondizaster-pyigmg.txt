<h1>IronDizaster's <cite>Playing Your Idle Game Maker Games</cite> series</h1>
<cite><a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLk6ZXmsEp_yOtWZ-o8X1B4TGX5sgk-21c">Playing Your Idle Game Maker Games</a></cite> is a YouTube series created by <a href="?a=irondizaster">IronDizaster</a> that consists of 4 episodes released between May 2024 and June 2025. The general structure of each video is that IronDizaster will play an IGM game and commentate his experience, before giving a verdict afterwards summarizing his thoughts and giving feedback. The first two episodes feature 4 games each, while the last two episodes focus on a single game each.
